Prepare your SD card ZIP

This app reads one ZIP made from your CPAP SD card. Keep the full folder structure intact so the app can find the files it needs.

How to make the ZIP

  1. Power off your CPAP and remove the SD card.
  2. Copy the full contents of the SD card into a folder on your computer.
  3. Optional: trim older day folders from DATALOG/ to shrink the ZIP.
  4. Create one .zip from that copied folder.
  5. Upload the ZIP here.

Agreement
